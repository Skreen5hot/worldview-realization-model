# Worldview Realization Model (WRM) — Formal Model v0.2

- **Working name:** Worldview Realization Model (WRM); the operator it formalizes is **the Dial**.
- **Status:** Formal model, v0.2 — supersedes v0.1; fully self-contained. Revised under the external architectural assessment of 2026-09-21, which endorsed v0.1 as the definitive conceptual baseline and identified four vulnerabilities (predicate-assignment fiat; inventory justification; generative confabulation at Layer 5; scenario authoring bias) and three formal ambiguities (ρ under-specification; the divergence metric; adjacency vs polarity). All seven are implemented here; dispositions in Appendix A. Still deliberately not a governance specification: no freeze acts, no panel machinery — instrument engineering arrives per investigation, only where an executed test needs it. **The research question, stated once for the team:** *we are not discovering worldviews from value dictionaries, and we are not declaring Steiner's cosmology dogma; we are testing whether modeling worldviews as predicate filters over a multi-relational problem graph generates distinct, coherent, and auditable pathways of value realization.*
- **Date:** 2026-09-21
- **Position:** A slice of the Integral Ethics Agent (IEA Architecture v2.1). WRM formalizes the mechanism behind *consulting a worldview*: not retrieving a stance's opinions, but re-filtering the situation's relational structure and re-deriving the problem configuration. WRM v0.2 is the program's live line; VWA v0.9 and VPWC v0.10 are formally sunset (§9), their findings ledgers intact and citable.

## 1. The ontological commitment, stated once

A value is a **bfo:Disposition** inhering in a moral agent (a material entity). Its core is worldview-invariant: Courage means the enduring disposition to face hazard, pain, or uncertainty for an ethically compelling good, under every dial setting. What varies with worldview is the **realization process** — the bfo:Process through which the disposition is realized in a concrete situation — because realization is governed by the agent's **act of appraisal**, and appraisal is where worldviews live. In Steiner's terms: moral intuition grasps the invariant concept; the percept supplies the situation; **moral imagination** maps concept-plus-situation to a concrete course of action, and the worldview is the orientation of that mapping. (The Thomist reading is one line away: the mapping is prudentia's work — the universal habitus applied to particulars; WRM does not adjudicate between the framings.)

WRM therefore formalizes worldviews as **operators on the problem configuration**, never as containers of values. Nothing in this model assigns values to worldviews, decomposes values into parts, or counts anything twelve. And the candidate inventory itself is held the way §6 states: as a **candidate spanning basis for human moral appraisal** — a coordinate system whose efficiency is measured against rivals and against chance — never as cosmology.

## 2. Formal objects

**2.1 The situation graph (Layer 1) and its completeness standard.** A scenario is a typed multi-relational graph **S = (E, R)**: E a finite set of entities, each typed against a pinned BFO/CCO release (material continuants, occurrents, roles, information content entities); R ⊆ E × Π × E a finite set of directed labeled edges, where **Π** is the situation-predicate vocabulary — object properties drawn reuse-first from the Relation Ontology, the CCO Extended Relations Ontology, and BFO relations, extended only where those demonstrably fail, each extension carrying a genus–differentia definition. S records what is the case; it is objective and shared; **the Dial never edits S.**

Two certifications guard Layer 1, because the Visibility Lemma cuts both ways — an author can starve a dial by omitting its domain, or favor one by seeding it:

- **Scenario firewall.** No worldview name, thesis, or cognate appears in E, R, or their documentation; a lexicon scan (inherited, §8) certifies this.
- **Multi-Relational Completeness (MRC) standard.** Π is stratified at the top by the **relational domain taxonomy D** — the ontology-native upper grouping of object properties (at minimum: physical/causal; agentic; informational; evaluative/experiential; legal/institutional), the same taxonomy §2.2 anchors the assignment in, so fairness is checked in the currency the dial spends. An MRC audit of S publishes the **per-domain edge-density profile** and certifies non-zero representation in every domain of D, or records a justified-emptiness argument tied to the scenario's reality (some situations genuinely lack, say, institutional structure). On an MRC-certified scenario, a family returning an empty or near-empty subgraph is a **substantive result about that family and that situation**, not an authoring artifact; a gross density imbalance is flagged in the audit even where non-zero.

**2.2 The dial: an externalized, perturbation-tested predicate assignment.** Each orientation w in the candidate inventory **W** is modeled as an **admissible predicate family Π_w ⊆ Π**. The family assignment **A : W → 2^Π** is a **cover with overlap, not a partition** — families may share predicates; disjointness would recommit, one level up, the error that sank the partition-based instruments.

A is **not authored by fiat**. It is:

1. **Anchored.** Every predicate enters Π carrying its ontology-native metadata — parent property, domain and range types, definitional axioms, and its domain in D. Family membership is assigned by **published criteria over that metadata** (range-type and relational-genus conditions per family), and every assignment carries a justification record citing those native features. Anchoring reduces discretion to the published criteria; the criteria themselves remain authored artifacts — this residue is measured, not denied, by (3).
2. **Externalized.** A is a **versioned, content-addressed configuration artifact** (`A.json`): inspectable, diffable, cited by hash in every result. Rival assignments — Pepper-shaped (4 families), Dilthey-shaped (3), and the **randomized null** A_rand (a random cover of Π at matched family count and sizes) — are constructed **by the same method under the same firewall** and versioned beside it.
3. **Perturbation-tested.** A frozen perturbation set moves **boundary predicates** — members of overlaps and near-boundary assignments — between adjacent families, and the D-1 results (divergence, lineage, activation profiles) are recomputed per perturbation. Stability within the frozen tolerance → the assignment's residual discretion is shown not to carry the result; instability → **ASSIGNMENT-FRAGILE**, reported as the finding.

**Adjacency, split in two** (the overlap-vs-polarity repair): **lexical adjacency** adj_Π(w, w′) = |Π_w ∩ Π_w′| / |Π_w ∪ Π_w′| is a constructional statistic only — predicate co-occurrence does not imply evaluative alignment, since two orientations can admit the same predicate (exerts_force_on) with opposite teleology, one amplifying and one neutralizing. The empirically meaningful adjacency is **operational adjacency** adj_op(w, w′): output similarity over benchmark scenarios — similarity of activated reference conditions, witness sets, and, decisively, **resolution polarity** on shared edges, read from the operation signatures of §2.5. All continuity claims (§7.1) are stated over adj_op; adj_Π serves only construction and perturbation bookkeeping.

**2.3 The filter operator.** **F_w(S) = (E, R_w)** with R_w = {(e, π, e′) ∈ R : π ∈ Π_w}. Entities are retained; only edges are filtered; the **in-scope subgraph** at w is the union of components of F_w(S) containing at least one edge. F_w is deterministic, inspectable, and idempotent.

**2.4 The layer functions — with ρ specified.** Turning the dial to w derives the **problem configuration** PC_w(S) = ⟨F_w(S), 𝓡_w, Δ_w, σ_w⟩:

- **Layer 2 — Reference-condition activation, formalized.** ρ is **pattern matching against a frozen library, not a black box.** A **reference-condition library L_R** is a versioned registry of prescriptive information content entities (CCO Prescriptive ICE, ont00000965) — specifications, regulations, well-being conditions, dignity and agency conditions — authored under the firewall, **scenario-independent** (built before or apart from any particular S), and itself MRC-audited: the library must carry standards across every domain of D, or a domain-starved library re-imports the Layer-1 bias one level up. Each r ∈ L_R carries an **applicability pattern** α(r): a typed graph pattern over entities and edges. Then **𝓡_w = ρ(F_w(S)) = { r ∈ L_R : α(r) matches within F_w(S) }** — deterministic, possibly plural. Dial-dependence is now a theorem-shaped fact rather than a stipulation: α(r) can only match on in-scope edges, so which standards activate covaries with Π_w. A Rationalist family surfaces institutional specifications; a Psychist family surfaces experiential well-being and trust; a Pneumatist family surfaces the preservation of autonomous agency and dignity — because those are the standards whose patterns their edges satisfy. Where 𝓡_w is plural, the downstream layers are computed **per activation**, and D-1 reports lineage per activation.
- **Layer 3 — Discrepancy** Δ_w = δ(F_w(S), r) for each activated r: the recognized deviation, formalized as a **witness set** — the minimal edge set (and prescribed-but-absent edge set) W_Δ ⊆ R ∪ R̄ whose presence/absence constitutes non-satisfaction of α(r)'s prescribed pattern — together with a descriptive ICE (ont00000853) recording it.
- **Layer 4 — Significance** σ_w = (Δ_w, o): the link from the discrepancy to an end — o an objective the agent holds (CCO directive-information family; IRI fixed at pin time), typed by the activated standard's domain: legal liability, bodily survival, degradation of conscience. Objectives are stated **in domain terms only**, never in orientation terms — a constraint §2.5 depends on.
- **Layer 5 — Response** p: a **process specification** — an explicit operational workflow (a typed process graph of acts, participants, and manipulated relations), proposed as a bfo:Process, bearing the realization link **p realizes d** for the value disposition d in play.

**2.5 Constrained, label-blind synthesis (the generative-leap repair).** The step from significance to response is where a language-model generator could confabulate — producing a compassionate-sounding response because a "Pneumatist" cue evokes the *style* of spiritual philosophy rather than because the filtered structure demanded it. Synthesis is therefore constrained at generation, not merely audited afterward:

1. **Blind packet.** The generator receives **only** the serialized ⟨F_w(S), 𝓡_w, Δ_w, σ_w⟩ — with **every worldview and family identifier stripped, end to end**: no orientation names, no family labels, no stylistic worldview cues; objectives in domain terms per §2.4. (The packet includes 𝓡_w and σ_w, not the subgraph and discrepancy alone, because a generator without the standard and the end cannot produce a well-posed response — the masking target is ideological identity, not the formal problem.) The packet's exact serialization is hashed into the record (§8, record rules).
2. **Workflow form with operation signatures.** p must be emitted as an operational workflow whose **target entities ⊆ in-scope entities**, whose steps address the witness set — every element of W_Δ is either resolved, mitigated, or explicitly deferred with reason — and whose edge manipulations are declared as an **operation signature** ops(p) ⊆ (E × Π × E) × {create, remove, amplify, attenuate, preserve}. The signature is what makes resolution **polarity** explicit and machine-comparable — the object adj_op and the divergence metric read.
3. **Placebo-filter control.** The same pipeline runs under A_rand's matched-size random family: outputs under a structureless filter should fail to activate coherent standards, fail witness-matching, or collapse into non-divergent generic responses. A placebo run that performs indistinguishably from real families is evidence the generator is drawing on priors, not structure — reported as the finding.

**2.6 The lineage audit — constraint plus check.** Every response carries its justification chain **J(p) = p → σ_w → Δ_w → r → W_Δ ⊆ R_w**, and **Audit(p, w)** passes iff: the chain is complete; it bottoms out **only** in Π_w-admissible edges of the frozen S; the blind-packet manifest confirms the generator saw nothing beyond §2.5(1); and ops(p) addresses W_Δ per §2.5(2). The audit is mechanical. Post-hoc rationalization is caught twice: a chain citing out-of-family edges fails here, and a chain that also grounds cleanly under a foreign dial fails the negative control of §5.

**2.7 Realization.** **Realize(d, S, w) = p** such that resolves(p, Δ_w) ∧ realizes(p, d) ∧ Audit(p, w) passes. The Dial is the map w ↦ PC_w(S); the disposition d is not an argument the dial can change.

## 3. The model's two commitments and one lemma

- **Invariance (Layer 1 and the disposition).** S is dial-invariant by construction; d is dial-invariant by ontological commitment (§1). If, empirically, coherent responses under different dials cannot be produced without redefining d per dial, **the invariance premise fails and the model is falsified at its foundation** — a registered falsifier, not an assumption to protect.
- **Covariance (Layers 2–4).** 𝓡_w, Δ_w, σ_w are functions of Π_w through pattern activation. Two agents at different dial settings, facing the identical S, can hold **irreconcilable problem definitions while agreeing on every fact in S** — the model's explanation of persistent ethical disagreement is this covariance, exhibited as differing activations and witness sets, not as differing values.
- **Visibility Lemma.** A discrepancy Δ with witness set W_Δ satisfying W_Δ ∩ R_w = ∅ is **invisible at w**: no standard whose applicability pattern matches only out-of-scope edges can activate, and no activated standard can be witnessed non-satisfied by edges outside R_w. Corollary (the blind-spot formalization): for any w whose family excludes a predicate domain genuinely present in S, there exist deviations that dial setting cannot recognize — the formal content of "moral blind spot," moral failure rendered as a filtering constraint rather than depravity, and the formal ground of Investigation 4's rotation hypothesis.

## 5. The Dial test (D-1) — the end-state proof target, formalized

D-1 is the single demonstration this project must deliver: *given one frozen problem space, turning the dial produces distinct, concrete, verifiable processes realizing the same value disposition differently.*

**Protocol.** (1) **Freeze the scenario:** one high-stakes S (the engineering whistleblower case is the working candidate), authored under the scenario firewall, **MRC-certified with its density profile published**, entities and edges BFO/CCO-verified, content-addressed before any dial is applied; L_R and `A.json` content-addressed likewise. (2) **Apply the dial** at 3–4 settings chosen **non-adjacent under adj_op's prior estimate** (working set: Materialism, Rationalism, Dynamism, Pneumatism) — non-adjacency matters, because continuity predicts *similar* outputs at adjacent settings, and testing divergence across neighbors would be unfair to the model's own geometry. (3) **Generate** PC_w(S) per activation and the response p_w per setting through the §2.5 blind-packet pipeline, with full lineage; run the placebo filter alongside.

**Success criteria — all three, formal.**

1. **Structural divergence, on a metric frozen before any credited run.** The metric is declared now, mathematically: a composite over the workflow specifications themselves — **(a)** Jaccard distance between target-entity sets; **(b)** Jaccard distance between manipulated relation-type sets; **(c)** typed graph edit distance between workflow process graphs under a frozen cost schedule; **(d)** polarity disagreement on shared edges, read from ops(p) — with a frozen aggregation. Its **divergence floor is calibrated, not chosen**: the same-dial repetition and paraphrased-scenario controls define the noise band (generation variance under an unchanged dial), and credited divergence must exceed that band — the calibrated-tolerance pattern inherited from the predecessor programs. Semantic paraphrase distance appears nowhere in the metric.
2. **Distinct value realization.** Each p_w bears realizes → d with a **different satisfaction condition** — what counts as the process having succeeded differs (technical isolation achieved; formal report filed and enforced; affected persons directly addressed) — while d itself is one disposition. If the outputs can only be made coherent by splitting d, record the §3 invariance falsifier.
3. **Traceable lineage, with negative control.** Audit(p_w, w) passes for every setting — blind-packet manifest included — **and** the chain J(p_w), evaluated under a different setting w′, fails its audit or grounds a different witness set: each response is licensed by its own filter, not by a shared rationalization reachable from anywhere.

**Companion reports, published with any D-1 result:** the **assignment-perturbation stability** record (§2.2.3 — stable, or ASSIGNMENT-FRAGILE); the **placebo-filter comparison** (§2.5.3); the MRC density profile; the per-activation lineage table.

**Recognition check.** Human experts judge each p_w a valid ethical pathway for the scenario — corroboration under the standing ceiling, never truth; run lightly, no eight-act machinery.

**D-1 falsifiers, enumerated:** the p_w converge within the calibrated noise band (the dial does nothing); divergence appears but lineage or the blind-packet manifest fails (rationalization or contamination, not appraisal); a dial setting yields no activatable standard on an MRC-certified scenario whose graph genuinely contains that family's domain (the family, or the library's coverage of it, is incoherent as built); the negative lineage control passes under foreign dials (filters not doing selective work); the placebo filter performs comparably (priors, not structure); ASSIGNMENT-FRAGILE at the frozen tolerance (the result rides on residual authorial discretion); invariance breaks per criterion 2. Any of these is reported as the result, not repaired.

## 6. Inventory stance: a candidate spanning basis, benchmarked

The twelve enter WRM as a **candidate spanning basis with circular topology** — a proposed coordinate system for human moral appraisal, evaluated on **coverage and efficiency**, not asserted as cosmic universals. The comparative apparatus is first-class:

- **Rival bases:** Pepper's four world hypotheses and Dilthey's three types, their assignments built by the §2.2 method identically.
- **Chance:** the randomized cover A_rand at matched budget.
- **Frozen comparative statistics:** **discrepancy coverage** — over the benchmark scenarios, the fraction of registered critical discrepancies visible under at least one family of the basis, at matched total predicate budget; and **compression loss** — discrepancies distinguished under the twelve that a coarser basis merges or misses. If the twelve capture critical structure that Pepper and Dilthey compress away, the framework is defended on empirical utility; if a coarser basis ties, that tie is the finding (the inventory-neutrality discipline inherited from the predecessor's mirror battery: the comparison must be shown fair to every cardinality before it decides anything).

**What the model still does not claim (carried verbatim in force):** it will not prove Steiner's twelve are the complete metaphysical truth; it will not automate moral authority or select the correct dial setting — it cannot tell an executive to take the Pneumatist path; it will not eliminate ethical disagreement — its ambition there is *explanatory* (§3, covariance); it is not an artificial general intelligence — it is an inspectable decision-support and deliberation structure. And, inherited unchanged: **no operational deployment inside IEA triage or deliberation before an Investigation-4-style validation on an independently scored task**; until then WRM is a research model.

## 7. What the model predicts (and where each prediction is tested)

1. **Continuity, over adj_op:** output similarity between dial settings decreases with operational-adjacency distance; the setting space should exhibit circular/manifold structure rather than collapsing to a few linear axes → **Investigation 3**, which runs first or in parallel, since its geometry defines what "coherent families" may even mean for **Investigation 1** (a cover with overlap and operational adjacency — the memo's original "non-overlapping" criterion remains amended).
2. **Family constructibility:** Π admits a principled, anchored assignment A covering the candidate inventory under the §2.2 method, stable under boundary perturbation — with rival and null assignments constructed identically, and the leakage-in-weights ceiling attached to any model-assisted construction → **Investigation 1**.
3. **Determinate divergence:** the blind-packet pipeline over a fixed MRC-certified S surfaces distinct, audit-passing realization strategies for a fixed d, exceeding the calibrated noise band, with the placebo filter failing → **Investigation 2**, whose instrument is D-1 at prototype scale and which the assessment recommends executing next.
4. **Deliberative utility:** systematic rotation through operationally non-adjacent settings reduces formally-defined blind spots (Visibility Lemma corollary) and yields more robust realization plans than a static value-lookup agent, on an independently scored task → **Investigation 4**, which is also the deployment gate.
5. **Derived triage (the IEA payoff).** Relevance stops being a stored matrix: **w is prima facie relevant to S iff F_w(S) activates at least one standard with a live discrepancy** — a per-scenario, computable property. The question VWA tried to answer as a static salience lookup returns as a byproduct of activation, and LOW-triage becomes auditable: a worldview is set aside exactly when its subgraph carries nothing to appraise, and that emptiness is itself inspectable against the MRC profile.

**Grounding note.** Whether the twelve are *real as predicate categories* is a grounding question. The Worldview Grounding Inquiry (protocol v1.0) remains the insulated, independent stream for it; WRM artifacts stay out of Inquiry reviewer packets, and convergence is evaluated only after both have independently produced results.

## 8. Inheritance from VWA and VPWC (kept deliberately short)

**Carried forward:** the firewall with lexicon scanning — now guarding scenario authorship, library authorship, and assignment construction; content-addressed pinning of every frozen artifact (S, `A.json`, L_R, the metric, the perturbation set); the evidentiary-record rules (verbatim output, script and output hashes, interpreter versions, reported-vs-reproduced marking) for every executed test, blind-packet manifests included; the control instinct — duplicate/paraphrase repetitions defining the noise band, the placebo filter, the negative lineage control, rival inventories as first-class comparisons; **calibrated tolerances over chosen thresholds** (the divergence floor, the perturbation tolerance — set from control behavior, the τ_C/S1 pattern); **comparison-fairness before comparison-verdicts** (the mirror-battery lesson, reborn as §6's matched-budget statistics); the honest-terminal vocabulary and the rule that falsifiers are reported, never repaired; the no-deployment-before-validation clause; owner ratification of load-bearing decisions. **Deliberately dropped:** value decomposition; incidence matrices; grain selection; quotients and tolerance partitions; the surjection/bijection apparatus; the salience matrix; the eight-act panel as a default. **Standing design test, kept:** before any investigation's result is credited, show on a constructed case that its method can recognize a genuine dial effect **and** fail honestly on a null — Battery II's lesson, which here is discharged by the placebo filter and the D-1 controls.

## 10. The end-of-project demonstration (the slice, concretely)

One artifact set, presentable in a working session: **(a)** the frozen scenario graph S, firewall- and MRC-certified, density profile shown, rendered inspectably; **(b)** the dial — an interactive control over the settings — showing, live, F_w(S), the activated standards from L_R, and the derived PC_w(S) at each setting; **(c)** the generated response workflows p_w side by side, each realizing the same named disposition d, with their operation signatures visible; **(d)** the lineage audit, clickable from each p_w back through σ → Δ → r → the exact edges in scope, with the blind-packet manifest attached and the negative control shown failing under foreign dials; **(e)** the D-1 scorecard: divergence-metric values against the calibrated floor, satisfaction-condition comparison, audit results, perturbation-stability record, placebo comparison, and the expert-recognition record. That artifact *is* the demonstration that a worldview, in this system, is a working appraisal operator inside the IEA's deliberation — a slice of the integral ethics system doing recognizable ethical work, with every claim inspectable and every failure mode allowed to show.
